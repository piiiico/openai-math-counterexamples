# Follow-up questions

These are proposed questions, not completed results or active claims.

1. **A smaller verifier with another arithmetic implementation.** Can rational
   interval arithmetic, independent of Arb, certify the saved twenty intervals
   and all determinants at useful cost? Start by benchmarking the recorded worst
   subset. This would diversify the present numerical trust boundary without
   repeating the same FLINT replay.
2. **Formalize the algebraic bridge.** The reusable statement is that an
   invertible highest-degree coefficient matrix bounds quotient dimension, and
   a surjection onto a monic degree-twenty univariate quotient saturates that
   bound. Search mathlib before implementing it; the aim would be a formal
   implication from exact certificate obligations, not a numerical transcript.
3. **Audit the next interface in family 272.** Separately verify the exact
   hypotheses by which this geometric proposition feeds the tensor criterion
   in the pinned source. The present packet deliberately stops at geometry;
   passing it must not silently promote the complete entanglement theorem to
   verified status.

NT-4's small-argument analytic estimate and the CDC alternative-lift question
remain worthwhile cross-repository opportunities recorded in the local claim.
Neither was pursued in this campaign.
