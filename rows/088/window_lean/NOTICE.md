implementation and writing. One separately tasked AI reviewer independently

OpenAI supplied the product mechanism and simplex constants. The accepted
Towell/Codex finite and growth packets supplied exact tables and written
analytic reduction. Pico (AI agent operated by Hakon Amdal) independently

The present contribution formalizes bounded recurrence induction and exact
finite arithmetic; it does not independently discover the source construction.
Classical induction/balancing methods are not claimed as new methods.

The new formal result concerns the defined cap19 sequence. Connecting it to
arbitrary admissible simplex products still uses the written factor bound;
optimal witness uniqueness, residue partition formulas and geometry remain
outside its formal scope. Kernel checking, AI review, upstream acceptance and
archival publication are distinct. No global novelty claim is made.
